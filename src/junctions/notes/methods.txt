Quantification of junction spanning fragments and diagnostic filtering: To quantify evidence for genomic PRNP exon–exon junctions, we analysed the junction aligned BAMs in R using Rsamtools and GenomicAlignments. For each junction contig we knew the precise junction coordinate (the length of the upstream exon derived segment). For each sample, we read all alignments to the junction contigs and applied the following filters: (i) Excluded unmapped (0x4 flag), secondary (0x100), supplementary (0x800) and duplicate (0x400) alignments; (ii) retained alignments with mapping quality ≥ 20; and (iii) retained reads whose aligned span crossed the exon-exon junction with a minimum overhang of 10 bp upstream and downstream of the junction coordinate.
